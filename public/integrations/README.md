# Integration logo assets

The SVG marks in this directory were sourced from the Simple Icons v16 distribution on 2026-09-27 and are used only to identify their respective integrations.

Simple Icons is distributed under CC0, but individual marks may remain subject to their owners' trademark and brand-usage rules. AgentFlow therefore keeps a generic icon fallback for brands whose asset is unavailable or unsuitable. See the [Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) before adding or replacing a mark.
